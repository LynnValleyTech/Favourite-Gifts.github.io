Our Story

Founded in 2010, Favourite Gifts has been North Vancouver's premier destination for unique and thoughtful gifts. What began as a small shop on Lonsdale Avenue has grown into a beloved community hub.

We're proud to showcase the work of over 50 local artisans and craftspeople, offering our customers truly special items they won't find anywhere else.

Our knowledgeable staff are passionate about helping you find the perfect gift for every occasion.
